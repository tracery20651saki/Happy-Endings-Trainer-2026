## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Happy Endings is primarily a management and clicker game rather than a character-movement game. Use the mouse to navigate the parlor interface, select rooms, manage services, start customer sessions, and access upgrades. The trainer does not add movement controls.

### Tools & Inventory

The game does not use a traditional combat inventory. Progress is organized around cash, customer rewards, rooms, services, upgrades, staff customization, heat management, and legacy bonuses. Activate resource-related options only after the relevant value is visible in the interface.

### World & Environment

Build the parlor from a small backroom into a larger adult business by purchasing rooms, improving services, hiring and customizing hostesses, raising the parlor tier, and unlocking additional scenes. Automation and rebirth upgrades support longer-term progression.

### Combat & Weapons

Happy Endings has no conventional combat system. Its active gameplay centers on pressure-point mini-games, where correctly timed actions satisfy customers, build streaks, and improve payouts. Combat-related trainer options are therefore not applicable.
